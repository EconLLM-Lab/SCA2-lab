## Contribution in plain language
The paper asks what a retained benchmark warrants. A conditioning method that keeps an external benchmark's order of countries has passed a test of the link between benchmark and method. Whether it says anything about survey respondents or about screening new items needs a further test, and the two links can fail independently. We build one method whose links can be separated (six declared country-level GPS signs, synthetic preference pairs, group unnamed at evaluation), test each link, and report a specific profile: training installs the labels on unseen pairs (construction fidelity); on trust the adapters keep the GPS order across a new scenario bank, and a prompt stating the same signs does too, so the signs carry that order and the weights are not credited; the dimensions are entangled; a preregistered retrospective screen gives a weak signal that registered contrasts do not separate from questionnaire readers; no dimension passes both registered checks; adapter-human agreement is not detected; the adapters sit farther from survey answer distributions than the base model while a country-named prompt sits closer. Not claimed as new: the measurement lens, the order/distribution distinction, entanglement, distribution-matching fine-tuning, country-persona prompting (all cited per NOVELTY_AUDIT). What is new: the construction's training signal and unnamed group, the reusable protocol, and the failure profile.
